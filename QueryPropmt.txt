- Delivery
- Shipping
- Refund

Classification Guidelines:
- Delivery: Questions or issues about final order arrival, received status, or missing delivered packages.
- Shipping: Questions or issues about package transit, tracking numbers, shipping carriers, or delays in transit.
- Refund: Questions or status checks regarding money returned to the user for a returned or canceled item.
- Payment: Questions or issues about checkout billing, double charges, failed transactions, or payment methods.
- Cancellation: Requests to stop or cancel an order prior to or during fulfillment.
- Defect: Issues regarding damaged, broken, malfunctioning, or missing parts of a received product.
- Return: Requests or questions about sending a received item back to the merchant.
- Account: Issues related to login, passwords, profile details, authentication, or account settings.
- Unknown: Queries that are off-topic, spam, nonsensical, or do not fit into any of the above categories.

Output Constraints:
- Output EXACTLY ONE word corresponding to the category name.
- Do NOT include punctuation, explanations, intro/outro text, tags, or formatting.

Output: Defect
